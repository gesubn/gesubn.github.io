🔍 What is SINdle?

SINdle is a small add-on for KoReader, a popular reading app for Kindle devices. Think of it as a secret room inside your Kindle. Once set up, SINdle creates a hidden library that only opens with a password. Your regular Kindle books stay where they are, and your private collection is tucked away behind a lock that only you can open.

The name says it all: SINdle is designed for the books you would rather not display on your home screen. It works quietly in the background and does not change how you read your everyday books. You get a clean, separate space for your most personal reading, without complicated settings or technical know-how.

🛡️ Why You Need SINdle

Most Kindle libraries are open for anyone who picks up the device. If you share your Kindle with a partner, a child, or a friend, your reading list is visible to them. SINdle solves that problem by hiding a chosen set of books behind a password. You decide what goes into the hidden library, and you decide who gets access.

Privacy matters, especially for reading. Maybe you enjoy a genre you would rather keep to yourself. Maybe you are reading a gift for someone and do not want them to see it. Or maybe you simply believe that your reading choices are your own business. SINdle gives you that freedom without needing cloud accounts, subscriptions, or internet access. Everything stays on your Kindle, under your control.

✨ Key Features

  • Password protection: Your hidden library stays locked until you enter the correct password.
  • Invisible on the home screen: Books inside SINdle do not appear in your normal KoReader library view, keeping your secret collection out of sight.
  • KoReader patch integration: SINdle is built as a KoReader patch, so it works with the KoReader app you already use on your Kindle.
  • Kindle-friendly: Designed for Kindle, Kindle Paperwhite, and other Kindle models that support KoReader.
  • Lightweight and fast: The patch adds only a tiny layer of protection and does not slow down your reading experience.
  • Works offline: No internet connection is required. Your privacy stays local to your device.

About me
======
Hi! I'm Kayla, a Political Science MA student at the University of Mannheim. I seek to study political communication in developing countries and high-stakes political participation by marginalised groups. To answer these questions, I aim to use causal inference designs and interpret them using mathematical, or formal, models.
